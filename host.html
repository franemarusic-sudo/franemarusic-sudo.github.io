The Host

Frane

Frane is a senior at Yale and the founder of Yalenoteca, a supper club built on the idea that the best conversations happen at the dinner table. This past summer, he took cooking classes in Tuscany and earned a pasta-making certification. He is drawn to the culinary traditions, techniques, and stories that make food such a rich part of everyday life.
