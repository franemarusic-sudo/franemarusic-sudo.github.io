The Host

Frane

Frane grew up in Croatia, where he first learned to cook in the family kitchen. By high school, he was hosting dinner parties of his own, bringing friends together over homemade meals. He's always enjoyed having friends over, experimenting in the kitchen, and cooking for a crowd.

After spending part of the summer in Tuscany taking cooking classes and earning a pasta-making certification, he's now a senior at Yale and the founder of Yalenoteca. Through Yalenoteca, he brings people together around the table, often introducing people who might not otherwise have met.
